How to Convert Units (Without Memorizing Tables)

One method for length, weight and volume — and the two temperature formulas.

Most unit conversions are the same operation: multiply by the conversion factor. The only trick is knowing the factor — and temperature, which needs its own formulas.

The factor method

To convert, multiply by a fraction equal to 1. Example: miles to kilometers — 1 mile = 1.60934 km, so 5 miles × 1.60934 = 8.05 km. Going the other way, divide (or multiply by the reciprocal): 10 km ÷ 1.60934 = 6.21 miles.

Factors worth knowing

  • 1 inch = 2.54 cm (exact)
  • 1 pound = 0.4536 kg
  • 1 US gallon = 3.785 liters
  • 1 meter = 3.281 feet

Temperature is different

Because the scales start at different zeros, temperature needs formulas, not factors:

  • °F to °C: (F − 32) × 5/9
  • °C to °F: (C × 9/5) + 32

Example: 98.6°F = (98.6 − 32) × 5/9 = 37°C.

Frequently asked questions

How do you convert miles to kilometers?

Multiply miles by 1.60934. For a quick estimate, multiply by 1.6.

What is the formula to convert Fahrenheit to Celsius?

(F - 32) x 5/9. For Celsius to Fahrenheit: (C x 9/5) + 32.

How many cm are in an inch?

Exactly 2.54 cm per inch, by international definition.
