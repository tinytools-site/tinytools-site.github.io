About TinyTools

Simple, free tools that respect your time and your privacy.

TinyTools started with a simple frustration: most online calculators are slow, covered in pop-ups, and demand an account before showing a single result. We build the opposite — lightweight tools that load instantly, give you the answer, and ask for nothing in return.

What we offer

Ten carefully built utilities covering everyday math (percentages, loans, BMI, age and date calculations), practical text tools (word counter, case converter, password generator), unit conversion and a Pomodoro focus timer. Every tool runs entirely in your browser.

Our principles

  • Free forever: no paywalls, no premium tiers, no sign-ups.
  • Private: your inputs are processed locally and never sent to our servers.
  • Fast: plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript — no heavy frameworks.
  • Accurate: standard formulas and verified conversion factors.

How we stay free

TinyTools is supported by advertising and occasional affiliate links. This keeps every tool free while letting us add new ones. If you have a suggestion for a tool you would like to see, please contact us.
